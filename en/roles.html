Fig. 05Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free exploratory meeting?
No. I answer every request in writing; the work starts with an agreed engagement.
Board of directors or advisory board?
A board of directors decides and carries legal responsibility. An advisory board advises, without that responsibility. Both have their place, depending on the company's stage.
How is the CEO advisory mandate billed?
Fixed fee. Scope, deliverables and price are set up front.
Do you name the companies you work with?
No. Never without their explicit consent.
What kind of companies do you work with?
Mostly growing Québec companies, 10 years old or more, in technology, manufacturing and services. Sometimes a promising startup.
